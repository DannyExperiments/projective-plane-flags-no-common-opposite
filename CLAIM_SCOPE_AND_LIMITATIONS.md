# Claim scope and limitations

- Theorem 1 constructs a qualifying set in each projective plane of finite order at least two, conditional on existence of the plane. It refutes the proposed uniqueness of order two in every plane of order at least three.
- Theorem 3 classifies the sets and counts them in PG(2,q) for prime powers q at least three. The finite-field nucleus bound supplies the Desarguesian restriction. The classification in arbitrary non-Desarguesian planes is outside this result.
- The order-two case recovers 56 sets of cyclic triangle flags. Each is represented by three choices of the construction parameters.
- The classical point configuration and nucleus theorem are credited. The literature review documents closely related results and the evidence limits for prior work. The manuscript makes no priority claim.
- The finite code and rotating incidence diagram corroborate the example and selected cases. The paper proves its general statements symbolically.
